# Threadly Design Reference — Extracted from Pitch Deck

## Brand Identity
- **Wordmark**: "THREADLY" in wide-tracked serif caps (Didot/Bodoni style), rose gold gradient
- **Monogram**: Elegant script "T" with needle/thread flourish, rose gold
- **Tagline**: "SMART STYLE. BETTER DEALS. ALL YOU." — spaced caps, warm white
- **Hero tagline**: "The AI stylist that shops smarter." — large serif, "smarter." in rose gold italic
- **Background**: Pure black (#0A0A0A) with subtle dark silk/satin texture suggestion
- **Divider**: Thin rose gold line with ✦ diamond center

## Color Palette (exact from deck)
- Background: #0A0A0A (pure black)
- Card surface: #1A1A1A (charcoal)
- Card border: rgba(201,149,106,0.2) (rose gold faint)
- Rose gold primary: #C9956A
- Rose gold light: #E8B89A  
- Rose gold gradient: #C9956A → #E8B89A
- Warm white: #FAF7F4
- Warm white muted: #C8C0B8
- Subtle text: #8A8078
- Success/deal green: #5DBF8A
- Blush accent: #F2D4C8

## Typography Hierarchy
- **Display/Hero**: Georgia or Didot-style serif, large (32-48px), warm white
- **Wordmark**: Tracked uppercase serif, rose gold
- **Section labels**: 10-11px, 1.5-2px letter-spacing, uppercase, rose gold
- **Body**: 14-15px, system sans, warm white / warm white muted
- **Captions**: 11-12px, subtle warm white

## App Screen Design (from deck mockups)
### Home Screen
- Top: "THREADLY" small wordmark + ✦ icon, centered or left
- Below: "AI RECOMMENDATION for you" label
- Large outfit image card (real fashion photography — blazer, black top, bag, shoes)
- "DEALS FOR YOU" section with product image thumbnails
- Bottom tab bar: Home, Closet, + (center), Looks, Shop — light/cream background in deck mockup

### Closet Screen (from deck)
- "YOUR CLOSET" header with item count
- Grid of clothing item images (real product photos)
- "CLOSET ANALYSIS" section: color swatches, style profile tags
- "Your style profile: Classic · Minimal · Feminine"
- "98% match" / "92% match" indicators

### Go New Screen (from deck)
- "GO NEW ✦" title
- "AI is building your look..."

- Checklist: "Analyzing your closet ✓", "Detecting current trends ✓", etc.
- "YOUR NEW LOOK IS READY ✦" CTA button (rose gold gradient)
- AI-Generated Look: flat lay of outfit items (blazer, black crop top, black pants, bag, sunglasses, earrings)
- "80% You already own 80% of this look." badge
- "Built from your closet first." label

### Shop/Deals Screen (from deck)
- "AI RECOMMENDED FOR YOU" header
- Outfit cards with real fashion photography

- "DEALS FOR YOU" section
- Deal cards: brand name, item name, original price struck through, sale price, % off badge
- "VIEW DEAL" button on each card

## Image Style
- Real fashion photography (not illustrations)
- Warm, moody lighting — amber/golden tones
- Neutral color palette clothing: camel, black, white, beige, cream
- Flat lay product shots for deals
- Model shots for outfit recommendations
- Closet/wardrobe photography for closet screen

## Key UI Patterns
- Cards: rounded corners (16-20px), subtle rose gold border, charcoal bg
- Badges: rose gold bg with dark text OR dark bg with rose gold text
- Buttons: rose gold gradient fill for primary, charcoal with rose gold border for secondary
- Progress: circular ring in rose gold
- Dividers: thin rose gold lines
- Icons: line-style, rose gold color
